Commercial Title Insurance

Protecting your commercial investment with the right policy — and the experience to handle even the most complex transactions.

Virtually any commercial property benefits from title insurance — office buildings, retail and shopping centers, industrial and warehouse space, multi-family and apartment complexes, mixed-use developments, and vacant or development land. If you are purchasing, refinancing, or investing in commercial real estate, a title policy protects your interest against defects, liens, and ownership disputes. Contact us to confirm the right coverage for your property type.
Commercial title insurance protects your investment against issues that may exist in a property's history — undisclosed liens, errors in public records, boundary and survey disputes, unknown heirs, or fraud. It gives lenders and owners confidence that the title is clear before closing, and provides financial protection if a covered claim arises afterward. Given the value at stake in commercial transactions, that protection is essential peace of mind.
There are generally two types of title policies: an owner's policy, which protects the buyer's equity in the property, and a lender's (loan) policy, which protects the lender's interest up to the loan amount. Who pays can vary by transaction, market custom, and negotiation between the parties. We'll walk you through the options and help structure coverage that fits your deal — reach out and we'll explain exactly what applies to your closing.
The process is simple: place your order with CTR Title, and we conduct a thorough title search to review the property's history. We identify and work to resolve any issues, then issue a commitment outlining the coverage. At closing, the policy is issued and your investment is protected.
